About Innovatech

Innovatech was born from a simple but powerful vision: to empower individuals and businesses through innovative technology and creative solutions. What began as a passion project by a team of tech enthusiasts has evolved into a dynamic digital agency committed to excellence, creativity, and results.

Founder’s Message

"At Innovatech, we believe that technology should not only solve problems but inspire new possibilities. As a team, we strive to push boundaries, innovate relentlessly, and provide solutions that shape the future. Every project we undertake is an opportunity to create something meaningful, something that can make a real difference in our client’s lives."

- David C. Igberi, Founder & CEO

At Innovatech, we specialize in delivering high-performance, secure, and visually appealing digital experiences. Our team offers end-to-end web development services, both front-end and back-end, crafting fully functional websites that are responsive, scalable, and tailored to your brand’s identity. Whether you’re launching a new idea or revamping an existing platform, we provide web solutions that communicate your mission, engage users, and drive growth.

But we don’t stop there. Our creative studio transforms ideas into reality through premium graphic design services. From logos and flyers to brochures, banners, and digital media assets, we ensure your brand is represented with visual consistency and artistic flair.

In the digital age, storytelling is key, and that’s where our video editing team comes in. We help clients turn raw footage into polished, engaging videos suitable for advertising, education, events, or social platforms. Clean cuts, captivating transitions, and a strong message are at the heart of every production.

Our video advertising service takes it further, creating short, compelling ads designed to capture attention and drive results. We handle everything from concept to production, ensuring your message reaches the right audience in the most impactful way.

Looking for smarter marketing tools? We offer custom QR code generation for business cards, event tickets, packaging, and promotional campaigns, bridging the physical and digital worlds with a single scan.

Innovatech also develops mobile applications that combine sleek design with seamless performance. We handle the complete app lifecycle from wireframing and coding to publishing on the Google Play Store and Apple App Store, ensuring your product is accessible and optimized for all users.

At Innovatech, every project is a collaboration, every idea a challenge, and every result a milestone. We are not just building websites or designing graphics, we are helping brands tell their stories, connect with their audience, and scale with confidence.

Let’s create something impactful together.
